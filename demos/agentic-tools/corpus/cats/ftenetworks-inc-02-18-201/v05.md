STRATEGIC ALLIANCE AGREEMENT  
“EDGE-FTE”

THIS STRATEGIC ALLIANCE AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”), made effective as of February 17, 2016, and continuing through February 16, 2019 (the “Initial Term”), is entered into by and between EDGE Communications Solutions, LLC, with its principal place of business at 6505 Windcrest Drive, Suite 200, Plano, TX 75024 (hereinafter referred to as “EDGE”), and FTE Networks, Inc., with its principal place of business at 999 Vanderbilt Beach Road, Suite 601, Naples, FL 34108 (hereinafter referred to as “FTE” or “Subcontractor”). This Agreement sets forth the terms and conditions under which FTE will supply labor, materials, equipment, supervision and/or services for its scope of work. FTE’s work shall be identified in a purchase order, if issued, which shall be subject to the terms of this Agreement (a “Purchase Order”). Each Purchase Order, together with the terms and conditions of this Agreement, shall constitute a separate agreement between the parties and shall be independent of any other agreement between the parties that incorporates the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

FTE agrees to perform its scope of work in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the applicable issued Purchase Order.

The Work, as defined below, is a portion of the services to be provided to Mediacom, LLC (the “Customer”).

1. TERMS

A. This Agreement shall remain in effect for the duration of the Initial Term, unless earlier terminated pursuant to the provision entitled “Termination of Agreement.” After the Initial Term, this Agreement shall continue on a month-to-month basis until terminated by either party upon thirty (30) days’ prior written notice to the other party, which notice shall state the effective date of termination. Termination of a Purchase Order shall not affect either party’s obligations under any other Purchase Order or this Agreement. Termination of this Agreement shall not, by itself, terminate any outstanding Purchase Order; each such Purchase Order shall remain in effect through completion of the applicable Work unless terminated in accordance with its terms or by mutual written agreement of the parties. Provisions of this Agreement or a Purchase Order that are expressly stated to survive termination, and obligations accrued before termination, shall survive.

B. If this Agreement expires during the period of performance of any Purchase Order, the remaining Work under that Purchase Order shall be completed under the terms of this Agreement in effect on the date the Purchase Order was issued, unless the parties mutually agree otherwise in writing.

C. Before performing or providing any labor, materials, equipment or services required to complete the work described in a Purchase Order, if any (the “Work”), Subcontractor must have a properly executed Agreement with EDGE and must have complied with the applicable terms and conditions of this Agreement, including submitting the required proofs of insurance. EDGE shall obtain any authorization from the Customer required under EDGE’s contract with the Customer to approve Subcontractor as a vendor and to approve the applicable Work terms and conditions. Subcontractor shall not commence Work until the applicable Purchase Order has been issued and any required Customer authorization has been obtained.

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, professional advisers, and contractors who need to know it for purposes of this Agreement and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those stated here; the Receiving Party remains responsible for their compliance.
