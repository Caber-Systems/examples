STRATEGIC ALLIANCE AGREEMENT  
“EDGE-FTE”

THIS STRATEGIC ALLIANCE AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”), made effective this 17th day of February 2016, through February 16, 2019 (the “Initial Term”), is entered into by and between EDGE Communications Solutions, LLC, with its principal place of business at 6505 Windcrest Drive, Suite 200, Plano, TX 75024 (hereinafter referred to as “EDGE”), and FTE Networks, Inc., with its principal place of business at 999 Vanderbilt Beach Road, Suite 601, Naples, FL 34108 (hereinafter referred to as “FTE” or “Subcontractor”), and sets forth the terms and conditions by which FTE will supply labor, materials, equipment, supervision and/or services for FTE’s scope of work. FTE’s work shall be identified in a written purchase order executed by both parties (each, a “Purchase Order”). No Work shall be required of FTE unless and until the applicable Purchase Order has been executed by both parties. Each Purchase Order, taken together with the terms and conditions of this Agreement, shall constitute a separate agreement between the parties and shall be considered independent of any other agreements between the parties that incorporate the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

FTE agrees to perform its scope of work in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the respective issued Purchase Order(s).

The Work, as defined below, is a portion of the services to be provided to a communications service provider, Mediacom, LLC (the “Customer”).

1. TERMS

A. This Agreement shall remain in effect for the duration of the Initial Term, unless earlier terminated pursuant to the provision entitled “Termination of Agreement”. After the Initial Term, this Agreement shall continue on a month-to-month basis until terminated by either party upon thirty (30) days’ prior written notice to the other setting forth the effective date of such termination. The termination of any Purchase Order shall not affect the obligations of either party to the other party pursuant to any other Purchase Order or to this Agreement. Termination of this Agreement shall prevent the issuance of new Purchase Orders but shall not, by itself, terminate any then-outstanding Purchase Order, which shall remain in effect in accordance with its terms unless terminated pursuant to its terms or by mutual written agreement of the parties. Obligations under this Agreement or any Purchase Order that are identified as surviving termination shall survive such termination.

B. Should the Agreement expire during the period of performance, all remaining Work under each Purchase Order will be completed under the terms of the Agreement in effect on the date of award of that Purchase Order, unless modified in writing by mutual consent of the parties.

C. Prior to performing or providing the labor, materials, equipment and services required to complete the work described in a Purchase Order, if any (the “Work”), Subcontractor must have a properly executed Agreement with EDGE and must have complied with all terms and conditions therein, including submission of the required proofs of insurance. EDGE shall obtain authorization from the Customer to approve Subcontractor as an approved vendor and to approve the Work terms and conditions of this Agreement if required under EDGE’s contract with the Customer. EDGE shall notify FTE in writing when any required Customer authorization has been obtained, and FTE shall not be required to commence the affected Work until it receives that notice.

2. SCOPE OF WORK

A. Subcontractor shall perform work for EDGE referred to as “Route Feasibility & Site Acquisition,” “Materials Management,” “OSP Construction,” and “Other Work,” as outlined in the attached Exhibits A, B, C and D and as further described in applicable Purchase Orders.

Any change to the scope, price, or schedule shall be documented in a written change to the applicable Purchase Order accepted by both parties. Purchase Orders shall be issued and administered as set forth in Section 3 below.

C. Unless otherwise provided in the Purchase Order, Subcontractor shall provide and pay for the materials and other facilities and services necessary for proper execution and completion of the Work, whether temporary or permanent and whether or not incorporated into the Work.

3. DISBURSEMENT OF PURCHASE ORDER

A. Work under this Agreement may be authorized from time to time by issuance of Purchase Orders to Subcontractor. Each Purchase Order shall reference and incorporate the terms and conditions of this Agreement, which shall apply to all Work performed under that Purchase Order. No Purchase Order shall obligate Subcontractor to perform Work unless accepted in writing by Subcontractor.

B. Nonexclusive Rights. This Agreement is an as-ordered agreement between EDGE and Subcontractor. EDGE may issue Purchase Orders for specific Work, potentially in amounts in excess of $20MM, but makes no commitment to issue any Purchase Order or to order any minimum amount of Work. 4. COMPENSATION & PAYMENTS

A. All Work shall be performed at the prices specified in the related Exhibit or in the applicable Purchase Order accepted by Subcontractor. Any additional or changed Work shall be compensated as agreed in a written change to the applicable Purchase Order.

B. Payment. EDGE shall pay Subcontractor for Work completed in accordance with the related Exhibit or applicable Purchase Order accepted by Subcontractor. Subcontractor shall submit invoices in the manner and with the supporting documentation specified in the applicable Purchase Order. EDGE shall pay undisputed amounts within thirty (30) days after receipt of a proper invoice and may withhold disputed amounts pending resolution, provided EDGE promptly notifies Subcontractor of the basis for the dispute and pays all undisputed amounts when due.

C. This Agreement shall not be construed as a “Pay When Paid” contract. EDGE’s obligation to pay Subcontractor under this Agreement shall not be predicated upon the Customer approving or paying EDGE for the Work.

D. Release and Waiver of Liens. Subcontractor shall pay for all services, equipment, materials, and labor used in performing the Work and shall keep EDGE’s and Customer’s property and work sites free of all claims or liens arising from Subcontractor’s performance. Upon reasonable request, Subcontractor shall provide customary lien waivers for amounts paid.
